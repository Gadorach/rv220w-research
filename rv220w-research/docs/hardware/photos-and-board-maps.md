## Evidence-preserving stitch policy

The canonical stitches are direct composites of the source photographs. Permitted operations are geometric alignment, homography/perspective correction, warping, overlap feathering, and cropping. The board content must not be regenerated, inpainted, completed, or semantically redrawn.

Lighting variations and stitch seams are acceptable. Accuracy is more important than cosmetic uniformity.

## Diagram policy

Annotated diagrams must use a direct stitched photograph as the board layer. Place legends and detailed text in whitespace outside the board whenever possible. Do not cover critical circuitry and do not replace package markings.

The two WLAN RF devices are recorded exactly as photographed:

```text
SiGe
2547A
2069CHF
```

Treat that as a transcription, not a verified full part number.

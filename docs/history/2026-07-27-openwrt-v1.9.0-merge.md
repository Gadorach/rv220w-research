# OpenWrt v1.9.0 merge — 2026-07-27

The original `rv220w-research` OpenWrt scaffold was replaced with the complete `rv220w-openwrt-promotion-toolkit-v1.9.0` tree.

The current-facing documentation was normalized around the hardware result reported for this build:

- complete OpenWrt boot through U-Boot/TFTP from RAM;
- all five RJ45 ports initialized and operational;
- LAN1–LAN4 through BCM53115 CPU port 8 and Octeon `eth0`;
- WAN through BCM53115 CPU port 5 and Octeon `eth1`;
- no LuCI integration;
- no operational Wi-Fi support;
- no persistent flash attempt or onboard NOR write.

The toolkit's version-specific stage and hotfix documents were retained as implementation history and regression evidence. Root and canonical topic documents now point to the v1.9.0 toolkit and clearly distinguish proven RAM operation from future persistent-installation work.

# RV220W v1.14.53 — point10 core1 gain-control rollback gate

## Qualified predecessor

v1.14.52 hardware-qualified point9 on CA channel 8. The transaction retained the qualified core0 gain and dedicated IQ calculation, core1 PHY `RFSEQCA=0x1221`, then programmed BCM2056r3 core1 calibration radio state with TXMUX/RXMUX `0x0006` and LNAG_TUNE `0x0007`. It reached MIPS `-ECANCELED=-158` and restored with cleanup rc/mask zero and exact coefficient rollback.

## New boundary

v1.14.53 exposes only point10. After the point9 radio setup, it calls the existing parameterized rev5-compatible gain helper with `rx_core=1`, therefore `tx_core=0`, then injects cancellation immediately after gain control. The dedicated core1 IQ tone/calculation and RCCAL remain unreachable.

The core1 gain telemetry is separate from the established core0 fields and requires a usable optimum, completed IQEST, corrected rotating 160-sample waveform, `RFSEQCA=0x1221`, BCM2056 mux/LNA route, and exact TX gain readback. Because opposite TX is core0, its BBMULT occupies bits 15..8 of N-table15/87; the point10 gate explicitly compares the expected BBMULT to that high byte.

Normal calibration, scan, association, AP mode, frame injection, user-data TX and persistent writes remain blocked.

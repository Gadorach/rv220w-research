# RV220W v1.14.48 — core0 gain RFSEQCA bitmap correction

## Hardware finding

v1.14.47 successfully generated a rotating 160-sample complex tone (`I_nonzero=160`, `Q_nonzero=128`) and kept sample playback active. TX gain and BCM2056 TX/RX calibration mux readback matched, yet IQEST returned zero for every raw accumulator. The captured `RFSEQCA` value was `0x1001`.

## Source comparison

Broadcom-derived `wlc_phy_rxcal_physetup_nphy()` uses four 4-bit bitmap fields in PHY register `0xa2` (`RFSEQCA`). For PHY rev < 7 it writes `(1 << tx_core)` into TXEN and RXDIS, and `(1 << rx_core)` into RXEN and TXDIS. The staged helper incorrectly wrote raw core indices. For the RV220W point-6 core0-RX/core1-TX path, stock-equivalent routing is therefore `0x2112`, not `0x1001`.

## v1.14.48 change

Only those four setup values change to `BIT(core)`. A target/analyzer readback gate requires `0x2112` at point 6. Existing phase/CORDIC/sample packing, sample-table rollback, IQEST rollback, gain-control constants, estimator wait mode, radio setup, forced-PIO policy, channel-8 restriction and dual acknowledgement are unchanged.

## Safety boundary

The maximum exposed fault point remains `core0-gain` (6). It permits the internal gain-search calibration tone and 1024-sample estimator, then cancels before the dedicated RX-IQ coefficient-calculation tone/calc step. Core1, RCCAL and persistent writes remain unreachable; normal `--rxiq-calibration` remains blocked.

# RV220W v1.14.27 — RX-IQ evidence correction and stock-calibration reconstruction checkpoint

## Hardware conclusion carried from v1.14.26

The v1.14.26 split diagnostic succeeded on hardware. Its software-only `nphy_calstate`
node returned `rc=0`, but accidentally emitted literal `\n` sequences instead of physical
newlines. The v1.14.26 analyzer therefore reported `calibration_state_present=FAIL` even
though the payload contains the complete state.

Recovered 2.4 GHz state:

```text
rx_iq_cal_rev3plus_implemented=0
rx_iq_cache_2g_a0_signed=0
rx_iq_cache_2g_b0_signed=0
rx_iq_cache_2g_a1_signed=0
rx_iq_cache_2g_b1_signed=0
iqcal_2g_center_freq=0
rssical_2g_center_freq=2412
nphy_calstate_rc=0
```

The restored v1.14.24 IQ estimator then completed on channels 1–11 and again measured
non-zero I/Q energy on both receive chains. This is the predefined signature
`rx-iq-calibration-unsaved-rssi-calibrated`: RSSI calibration state is saved for channel 1,
while rev3+ RX-IQ calibration has no saved chanspec or coefficients.

## v1.14.27 correction

- `nphy_calstate` emits real newline characters.
- The analyzer also normalizes the historical v1.14.26 escaped single-line payload, so the
  already-collected evidence can be reanalyzed without another RF run.
- The hardware-proven `nphy_rxdiag` implementation is unchanged.
- No RX-IQ calibration, RF override, calibration tone, scan, association or AP operation is
  added in this release.

## Why active RX-IQ calibration remains blocked

The preserved stock `wl.ko` contains `wlc_phy_cal_rxiq_nphy` (size 0x0d98), along with
`wlc_phy_rx_iq_coeffs_nphy`, `wlc_phy_rx_iq_est_nphy`, `wlc_phy_tx_tone_nphy`, RF-sequence
and carrier-search helpers, and pre-rev7 2.4/5 GHz RX-calibration gain tables.

Relocation/disassembly analysis of the stock routine also shows calls to unnamed local helpers
at `.text+0x11d370`, `.text+0x0f0b50`, `.text+0x0f5560`, `.text+0x10d758`, and
`.text+0x0fa7e8`. These calls occur in the state-save, gain/loopback, tone/estimator and
cleanup region. Not all can yet be mapped with enough confidence to reproduce the BCM2056
radio-rev3 setup/rollback path safely.

An active experiment will not be enabled until those radio-state-changing helpers are mapped
or replaced by an independently verified equivalent sequence. This prevents a diagnostic
release from guessing at BCM2056 loopback/power/override restoration.

## Qualification boundary

v1.14.27 is evidence-correction/reconstruction only. It does not qualify normal frame RX,
active scanning, association, AP mode, or rev3+ RX-IQ calibration.

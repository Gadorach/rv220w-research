# RV220W v1.14.46 — core0 gain corrected sample packing

## Why this revision exists

v1.14.45 proved that point 6 was not failing because playback stopped, TX gain was mis-programmed, the obvious BCM2056 calibration muxes were wrong, or IQEST timed out. Playback was active, live gain and loopback readback matched, IQEST completed, yet every raw accumulator was zero.

Inspection of the b43 N-PHY sample loader exposed a concrete packing defect relevant to this path: its I component is masked against the already-shifted field mask instead of masking the signed low 10 bits and then shifting them into bits 10..19. With ordinary calibration amplitudes this can collapse most positive I samples to zero and negative samples to essentially the sign-field residue.

## v1.14.46 change

The generic b43 loader is intentionally left untouched. Only the RV220W rev4 point-6 gain-search diagnostic uses a local waveform generator/loader that preserves the existing CORDIC frequency/amplitude sequence and packs each table word as:

```c
pi = (u16)iq.i & 0x03ff;
pq = (u16)iq.q & 0x03ff;
data[i] = ((u32)pi << 10) | pq;
```

The helper records sample count, nonzero-word count, nonzero I/Q counts, OR/XOR summaries and sample 0 before starting the existing non-IQ sample playback engine. Point 6 remains the maximum reachable fault stage; dedicated RX-IQ coefficient calculation, core 1, RCCAL and normal calibration remain blocked.

## New rollback surface

Tone-active tests overwrite N-PHY table 17. v1.14.46 therefore snapshots all 320 32-bit sample-table entries before any point-6 calibration mutation, stops playback during cleanup, restores the exact table image and verifies every word. A mismatch sets `RV220W_RXIQ_CLEAN_SAMPTAB` and fails qualification. Pre-tone points do not allocate or capture this table.

## Qualification interpretation

A successful run must still reach the architecture-resolved injected cancellation (`-158` on the MIPS target), with cleanup rc 0, cleanup mask 0, MAC suspend/resume verified and IQ before/after identical. The analyzer additionally requires a plausible corrected waveform for the v1.14.46 policy. If the corrected waveform is present but IQEST remains all-zero while playback/gain/routing remain good, the remaining issue is downstream of sample packing and point 6 stays unqualified.

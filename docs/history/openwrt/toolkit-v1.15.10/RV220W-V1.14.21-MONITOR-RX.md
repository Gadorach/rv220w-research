# RV220W v1.14.21 — fixed-channel monitor RX diagnostic

## Motivation

v1.14.20's 2.4 GHz passive cfg80211 scan returned success (`rc=0`) with b43 initialized and no association/probe requests, but the scan result set was empty. That result alone cannot distinguish a lack of nearby beacons during the scan from a receive/channel problem or a cfg80211 BSS-cache issue.

## Diagnostic

`monitor-rx` creates one monitor interface and performs no scan request. For each channel it sets the monitor channel, waits one second for settling, observes two seconds of traffic, and records both `/sys/class/net/rvwmon0/statistics/rx_packets` deltas and `tcpdump-mini` textual receive summaries. No pcap file or injected frame is created.

Scopes:

- `--band 2g`: 2412..2462 MHz, channels 1-11.
- `--band 5g`: 5180/5200/5220/5240 MHz, non-DFS channels 36/40/44/48 only.

A PASS means raw monitor RX activity was observed independently of cfg80211's BSS cache. If v1.14.20 still had zero BSS entries on the same band, the next investigation should focus on mac80211 scan/BSS handling. A zero-RX result remains diagnostic rather than proof of RF failure; it should be repeated on a band/channel with a known nearby beacon before deeper PHY/RX changes.

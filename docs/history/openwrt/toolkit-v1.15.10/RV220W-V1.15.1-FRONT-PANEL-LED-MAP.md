# RV220W v1.15.1 front-panel LED map

The v1.15.0 guided stock-U-Boot GPIO survey established the remaining user-visible front-panel LED GPIOs while restoring the exact Octeon `BIT_CFGX` state after each test.

## Qualified mappings

| Front-panel indicator | Octeon GPIO | Polarity | Survey observation | Linux label |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| POWER (green) | 2 | active-low | HIGH off, LOW on | `rv220w:green:power` |
| DIAG (red) | 3 | active-low | HIGH visually off, LOW on | `rv220w:red:diag` |
| WIRELESS (green) | 4 | active-low | HIGH visually off, LOW on | `rv220w:green:wlan` |
| DMZ (green) | 6 | active-low | HIGH visually off, LOW on | `rv220w:green:dmz` |

WIRELESS independently confirms the earlier stock `scm-led.ko` reconstruction. Its Linux default trigger remains `phy0tpt`.

POWER is already lit before Linux takes ownership, so its `gpio-leds` node uses `default-state = "keep"` to avoid an unnecessary boot-time transition. DIAG and DMZ default off and remain normally configurable through OpenWrt `/sys/class/leds`/LuCI LED configuration.

## GPIO5 exclusion

GPIO5 is **not an LED mapping**. Pulling GPIO5 LOW in U-Boot simultaneously extinguished the LAN1, LAN2, LAN3, LAN4 and WAN LED groups. Those groups are otherwise automatically controlled by the BCM53115/PHY hardware, making GPIO5 more consistent with a shared switch reset, LED enable, or related Ethernet-control signal than a front-panel LED.

Until that signal is independently reconstructed, v1.15.1:

- does not reference GPIO5 anywhere inside `gpio-leds`;
- marks GPIO5 sensitive in the U-Boot survey;
- skips it by default unless `--include-sensitive` is supplied;
- preserves the observation only as historical hardware evidence.

GPIO1 remains the known reset/recovery input and is likewise skipped by default.

## Production DTS

```dts
rv220w-leds {
        compatible = "gpio-leds";

        power {
                label = "rv220w:green:power";
                gpios = <&gpio 2 GPIO_ACTIVE_LOW>;
                default-state = "keep";
        };

        diag {
                label = "rv220w:red:diag";
                gpios = <&gpio 3 GPIO_ACTIVE_LOW>;
                default-state = "off";
        };

        wlan {
                label = "rv220w:green:wlan";
                gpios = <&gpio 4 GPIO_ACTIVE_LOW>;
                linux,default-trigger = "phy0tpt";
                default-state = "off";
        };

        dmz {
                label = "rv220w:green:dmz";
                gpios = <&gpio 6 GPIO_ACTIVE_LOW>;
                default-state = "off";
        };
};
```

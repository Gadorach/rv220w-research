## Visible functional zones

- **Upper left:** Ethernet magnetics and large silver heatsink, likely BCM53115 switch.
- **Centre:** Black heatsink, likely CN5010 SoC.
- **Right centre:** U6/U12 DDR2 memory.
- **Left centre:** U4 parallel NOR, U3 latch, 25 MHz clocks, and associated bus routing.
- **Lower right:** J2 2×7 test-pad header.
- **Lower left:** JP1 and JP2 1×4 through-hole headers.
- **Bottom edge:** Front-panel LEDs and associated transistor/resistor arrays.

## Required missing photography

Capture the following before continuity work:

1. Full-resolution board underside, perfectly square to the PCB.
2. Oblique photos showing via transitions around J2, JP1, JP2, U4, and U3.
3. Connector-side close-ups with port numbering.
4. WLAN daughter-card front and back.
5. Main power-input and regulator areas.
6. Heatsink-removed CPU and switch markings, if removal can be done without damage.

Use a ruler or known pitch feature in at least one image to permit coordinate scaling.

The script performs the equivalent of the following sequence after identifying
the exact live sector state:

base 0
flinfo
crc32 0xbdc00000 0x20000
md.b 0xbdc13138 4
md.b 0xbdc13168 4

# Exact pre-write rollback image:
cp.b 0xbdc00000 0x05700000 0x20000
cmp.b 0xbdc00000 0x05700000 0x20000

# Separate combined target:
cp.b 0x05700000 0x05500000 0x20000
mw.b 0x05513138 0x10 1
mw.b 0x05513139 0x00 1
mw.b 0x05513169 0xe0 1
crc32 0x05500000 0x20000
md.b 0x05513138 4
md.b 0x05513168 4

protect off 0xbdc00000 +0x20000
erase 0xbdc00000 +0x20000
cp.b 0x05500000 0xbdc00000 0x20000
cmp.b 0x05500000 0xbdc00000 0x20000
crc32 0xbdc00000 0x20000
md.b 0xbdc13138 4
md.b 0xbdc13168 4
protect on 0xbdc00000 +0x20000
